SERVICE AGREEMENT

1. Parties
This agreement is entered into between Party A and Party B.

2. Term
The agreement starts on 1 January 2026 and has a term of 12 months.

3. Services
Party A will deliver software development services as described in Appendix 1.

4. Payment
Party B shall pay invoices within 30 days of the invoice date.
Late payments accrue interest according to applicable law.

5. Confidentiality
Both parties shall keep confidential information secret during the term of the agreement.

6. Governing law
This agreement is governed by Norwegian law.
