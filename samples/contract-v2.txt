SERVICE AGREEMENT

1. Parties
This agreement is entered into between Party A and Party B.

2. Term
The agreement starts on 1 January 2026 and has a term of 24 months.
Either party may terminate the agreement with three months written notice.

3. Services
Party A will deliver software development services as described in Appendix 1.

4. Payment
Party B shall pay invoices within 14 days of the invoice date.

5. Confidentiality
Both parties shall keep confidential information secret during the term of the agreement and for two years thereafter.

6. Governing law
This agreement is governed by Norwegian law.
